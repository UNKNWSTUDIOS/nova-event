## Act 2 · Quirks (19:15–19:35) · 09–11
09 what you own — 5 min. Webflow: static pages only (most honest). Framer: nothing. Wix: nothing. Nobody exports interactions.
10 take / leave — 5 min. Content, originals, URLs, decisions, screenshots. Leave ALL the code. "Rebuild beats untangle, every time."
11 where migrations die — 8 min. Three per platform. Ask the room which platform they're on; go deeper on that one from memory.

## Act 3 · How we run it (19:35–20:00) · 12–19

13 audit stage — 2 min. Let it type. "Day one is counting. 14 font sizes → 6."
14 brand becomes code — 4 min. /ds on screen. One source, no drift, same rules for everyone, rebrand = one file.
15 taste written down — 4 min. Read two rules aloud. "Institutional memory. The 'not like that' from March holds in December."
16 eight things — 3 min. "Forty pages, all compositions of these. Change the button once — the thing the builder promised."
17 briefing — 3 min. Adjectives vs evidence. "Same brief a good freelancer needs. Not a coincidence."
18 page stage — 2 min. Let it type; point at "compare → 40 vs 48 → fix → ✓".
19 result — 1 min. unknw.com, ~40 pages, one designer runs it.
